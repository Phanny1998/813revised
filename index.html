The situation

An e-commerce team wants to offer next-day delivery for a larger part of the product portfolio. The idea is attractive for customers, but some products are stored only in central warehouses, some have unstable demand, and transport capacity is limited during peak periods. Customer service likes the promise while logistics is unsure whether the network can support it reliably.

Your task

Write one strong question for each LUNA dimension before this decision is approved.

About the feedbackInstant, rule-based checks look at question wording and relevant terms. Use them to refine your question; they don’t assess every possible valid answer.
